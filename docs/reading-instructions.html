The reading session

Häfner, Stock & Oberst (2014) — Decreasing students' stress through time management training

⏱ At least 15 minutes

The Finish button becomes available once you have spent 15 minutes reading. The clock in the header shows your reading time. Take as long as you need beyond that.

⏸ Pausing

If you need to step away, use the Pause button. Paused time does not count toward the 15 minutes, and it does not count against you. Feel free to take time as much as you can.

🧰 Tools on the right

✅ Finishing

When you are done, press Finish. You will not be able to return to the article afterwards. Make sure you understand the article before you finish.

Please only use the tools provided on this platform.

No external AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). No external PDF viewers or apps. Do not close this window during the session.
